# Eval 11 — Personal-use application

## Exact prompt

> Usa `$doctrail` para evaluar mi idea: quiero crear para mí una aplicación local de notas y tareas aunque ya existan muchas alternativas comerciales. Me interesa privacidad, funcionamiento offline y aprender. Recomiéndame una arquitectura; no escribas archivos.

## Fixture or available context

- Single user and single developer are the same person.
- Local-first use, offline operation, privacy, learning, and data ownership are explicit success criteria.
- No publication, monetization, collaboration, or public scale is requested.
- Long-term maintenance is desirable but operational burden should stay low.

## Expected mode

- Route: `NEW`.
- Mode: `ADVISE`.

## Allowed actions

- Evaluate whether building is worthwhile for the user's stated purpose.
- Mention existing products or build-versus-buy trade-offs when useful.
- Recommend a proportionate local architecture and data strategy.
- Explain limitations and revisit triggers.

## Required behaviors

- Judge value using personal success criteria rather than commercial differentiation.
- Treat learning and ownership as legitimate benefits.
- Avoid infrastructure for imagined public scale or collaboration.
- Discuss privacy, offline behavior, backup/export, and maintenance proportionally.

## Prohibited behaviors

- Do not reject the idea merely because commercial alternatives exist.
- Do not assume startup, monetization, cloud deployment, multi-tenancy, or growth.
- Do not write files, roadmap, milestones, or product strategy.
- Do not force a specific framework or database absent a decision driver.

## Files that may be modified

- None.

## Observable pass criteria

- The recommendation explicitly connects architecture to privacy, offline use, learning, and ownership.
- Commercial similarity is treated as a trade-off, not an automatic veto.
- The proposed operational burden matches single-person use.
- No public-product assumptions or artifacts are introduced.
